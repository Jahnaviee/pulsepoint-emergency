Our Medical Services

Emergency support, specialist consultations, and patient-focused care for every stage of life.

Emergency & Trauma Care

Our emergency team provides initial assessment and stabilization for road accidents, serious injuries, fractures, and sudden medical emergencies. Patients needing advanced specialist treatment may be referred to an appropriate facility after clinical assessment.

For emergencies: Contact local emergency services or proceed to an emergency department.

Pediatric Emergency Care

Children experiencing breathing difficulties, severe fever, dehydration, injuries, or other urgent symptoms need prompt medical assessment. Our sample service information describes initial evaluation and emergency support for infants, children, and adolescents.

Note: Pediatric emergency staffing and facilities must be confirmed with the hospital.

Maternity Emergency Care

Pregnancy-related emergencies, including heavy bleeding, seizures, severe abdominal pain, or other serious symptoms, require immediate medical attention. Report to the emergency reception for guidance to the appropriate maternity team.

Entrance: Ask emergency reception to direct you to maternity triage. A dedicated maternity entrance has not been verified.

Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Critical care may involve close monitoring and specialized support for patients with life-threatening conditions. ICU admission depends on clinical need, available equipment, staffing, and bed availability.

Availability: Confirm ICU facilities and current bed availability directly with the hospital.

Outpatient Department (OPD)

Outpatient consultations can help patients discuss health concerns, receive medical assessments, and arrange follow-up care with the appropriate specialist.

Sample timings: Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Doctor schedules may vary.

Appointment Information

General Medical Consultations

General consultations may include assessment of common illnesses, preventive health advice, and referrals for specialist evaluation when required.

Before visiting: Contact reception to confirm consultation hours and availability.

When Should You Seek Emergency Care?

Emergency

Possible stroke, severe breathing difficulty, major bleeding, unconsciousness, or serious injury. Call emergency services immediately.

Urgent Medical Concern

Symptoms that need prompt medical assessment but are not immediately life-threatening. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for guidance.

Planned Consultation

Routine checkups, follow-up visits, and non-urgent health concerns can usually be arranged through the outpatient department.
